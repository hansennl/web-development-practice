PCU HYMN

Fulfillment of our yearning, For Christian higher learning, To thee our eyes are turning PCU; Thy blue and silver banner In firm unchanging manner Is steadied by unshaken loyalty, REFRAIN: Thine is love of man in service tendered, Thine is love of God in worship rendered, Thine is wholesome growth in Christ engendered, Alma Mater, PCU
