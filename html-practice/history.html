The Origin of the Land of Dawn

2016

Official launch of Mobile Legends in SEA.

  • July 2016: Developer Moonton releases Mobile Legends: 5v5 MOBA on Android (and later iOS in November).
  • Core Roster: The game debuts with a small, iconic roster of early heroes including Layla, Miya, Alucard, Tigreal, and Eudora.
  • Mission: At a time when Multiplayer Online Battle Arenas (MOBAs) were strictly PC-dominated, MLBB introduces fast 10-to-15-minute matches and intuitive virtual dual-joystick controls designed specifically for smartphones.

2017

Southeast Asian Explosion

  • Regional Dominance: The game's popularity surges massively across Southeast Asia—particularly in the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Myanmar.
  • Name Rebrand: The title is officially rebranded to Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB).
  • First Official Tournaments: Moonton hosts its first international tournament, the MSC (Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Southeast Asia Cup) in Jakarta, Indonesia, laying the groundwork for professional mobile esports.

2018

The Birth of MPL Franchises

  • League System: Moonton establishes the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Professional League (MPL) in countries like Indonesia and the Philippines, creating structured, professional competitive seasons.
  • Roster Expansion: The hero pool grows rapidly, introducing unique mechanics like stance-switching, energy gauges, and assassin-heavy playstyles that raise the competitive skill ceiling.

2019

The First World Championship (M1)

  • M1 World Championship: In November 2019, Moonton hosts the inaugural M1 World Championship in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Indonesian powerhouse EVOS Legends claims the first-world title
  • 3D Visual Upgrades: The game receives major graphical and UI enhancements, replacing older 2D character models with fully animated 3D hero previews.

2020

Project NEXT & Global Esports

  • Project NEXT (Phase 1): Moonton launches its largest technical overhaul to date. Classic "starter" heroes like Miya, Layla, Eudora, and Zilong receive completely revamped skill kits and updated visual designs to keep them competitive in modern play.
  • Laning System Overhaul: The map layout is officially split into dedicated Gold Lane and EXP Lane setups, defining modern role strategies.

2021

The M2 & M3 Era

  • M2 World Championship (January): Hosted in Singapore under strict safety protocols, Philippines team Bren Esports wins the M2 trophy, kicking off an era of Philippine dominance in international MLBB esports.
  • M3 World Championship (December): Held in Singapore, Blacklist International wins M3 with their iconic "Ultimate Bonding Experience" (UBE) team-fight strategy.

2022

Talent Synergy & Project NEXT Evolution

  • Emblem Rework Preparation: Moonton begins testing major balance passes and revamped talent systems to give players more strategic freedom in pre-game loadouts.
  • M4 World Championship: Held in Jakarta, Indonesia, ECHO defeats reigning champions Blacklist International in a 4-0 sweep, solidifying back-to-back world titles for the Philippine region.

2023

Global Expansion & New Roster Milestones

  • Emblem System Overhaul: The classic emblem system is completely revamped into an intuitive, flexible talent tree, allowing any hero to equip customizable passive traits (e.g., movement speed, lifesteal, extra gold).
  • M5 World Championship: Hosted in the Philippines, AP.Bren wins M5, making them the first esports organization in MLBB history to win two M-series World Championship titles.

2024-Present

The Mid-Season Cup & Global Esports Era

  • MSC Rebrand: The Southeast Asia Cup officially expands globally into the MLBB Mid-Season Cup (MSC) with record-breaking prize pools, bringing in top teams from North America, Europe, Latin America, and the Middle East.
  • Continued Project NEXT Overhauls: Ongoing annual graphics upgrades, equipment rebalances, and continuous hero redesigns keep MLBB among the most played and watched mobile esports titles in the world.
