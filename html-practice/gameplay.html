Master the Land of Dawn

Map Layout

  • Three Main Lanes: Top Lane, Mid Lane, and Bottom Lane. Computer-controlled minions spawn periodically from each base and march down these lanes toward enemy towers.
  • The Jungle: The area between the lanes filled with neutral monsters (creeps). Defeating these gives extra gold, experience, and temporary buffs.
  • The Turtle: A boss creep in the river that grants gold and shields to the entire team when slain.(major objective)
  • The Lord: The ultimate boss creep. Defeating the Lord summons him to fight alongside your team, marching down the weakest enemy lane to crush their defenses.(major objective)

Hero Roles

ROAM

  • Initiates team fights, protects teammates, and absorbs incoming damage.

EXP

  • Balanced melee brawlers who can sustain damage while dealing solid output.

JUNGLE

  • High-mobility burst dealers who hunt down fragile enemy carries.

MID

  • Magic-damage dealers specializing in area-of-effect crowd control or burst damage from afar.

GOLD

  • Continuous physical damage dealers who start weak but scale into powerful late-game carries.

Match Flow & Mechanics

  • Farming & Levelling: During the early game, players earn gold and experience by defeating minions, jungle creeps, and enemy heroes.
  • Item Building: Gold is spent buying equipment that boosts your hero's physical attack, magic power, attack speed, defense, or movement.
  • Team Fights & Pushing: As heroes gain their ultimate abilities (Level 4), teams gather to coordinate ambush plays ("ganks"), fight over river objectives, tear down defensive turrets, and push straight into the enemy base for the final victory.
